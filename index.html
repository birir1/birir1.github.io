About

Building more trustworthy AI systems.

I am a Master's student in Computer Engineering at Chosun University, South Korea, specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing.

My research focuses on improving the reliability and trustworthiness of large language models, particularly in retrieval-augmented generation systems.

I am interested in hallucination mitigation, semantic verification, uncertainty estimation, adaptive retrieval, and multi-agent AI systems.
